# CDC and USWDS evidence audit

Checked: **2026-09-28**. This audit examines a proposed CDC/USWDS integration approach against public primary sources. It does not establish an official joint certification scheme or authorize adopting an agency brand.

## Result and scope

Most concrete palette, component, release, and Sass facts checked below have primary-source support. The central CDC/USWDS authority split is the report author's integration proposal. The reviewed authorities do not establish an official dual-conformance certification or universal healthcare design stack.

The portable Healthcare Software UX skill therefore includes a short optional government-systems reference. General Scientific Software UX and general healthcare work do not inherit CDC branding, its WCMS conventions, USWDS dependencies, or federal obligations merely because of their subject matter.

## Implementation examples: limits of this audit

The HTML/CSS/Sass fragments were read and compared with documentation. **No package installation, Sass compilation, browser execution, screen-reader test, or CDC WCMS integration test was performed.** They are not included as a tested starter application in the portable skill.

- The form has `novalidate`, which suppresses interactive constraint validation on submission, while no complete substitute handler/server flow is supplied. `required` and `type="email"` alone do not make this particular form a functioning validation implementation. Preserve native validation or provide and test the complete alternative, including server validation. See the [HTML Standard](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/form-control-infrastructure.html#attr-fs-novalidate).
- The invalid-field fragment shows one `role="alert"` per message. That can cause competing announcements if generalized to many fields. USWDS supports accessible error notification, including a changed page title, but this audit did not establish a reliable multi-error announcement/focus strategy for those snippets. See [USWDS form guidance](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/form/).
- The font custom properties are declarations, not font downloads, `@font-face` registration, or a complete mapping into USWDS typography. Placeholder logo paths, application routes, submission endpoints, assets, and JavaScript setup need actual implementation before a page works.
- A USWDS government banner must not be added to a public portfolio, unaffiliated demo, or ordinary healthcare website. Its domain, HTTPS, and official-site scope matters; government styling is not an authenticity mechanism. See [banner usage guidance](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/banner/).
- Preserving upstream markup and using a single behavior owner are sensible integration practices. They do not establish that a customized component is accessible or that its behavior fits a clinical workflow. Validate the assembled service, not only isolated components.

## Evidence provenance and remaining uncertainty

This audit uses explicit public URLs, including the HTML Standard, WAI alert-pattern, and Revised Section 508 sources for the corrections above. This is a targeted audit of material claims, not a claim that every sentence and example has been independently proved.

Unresolved project facts: the governing CDC template generation; current internal brand/WCMS approvals; exact CDC viewport thresholds and shell contracts; applicability of internal typography guidance; and the target product's jurisdiction, contracts, clinical risks, and acceptance criteria. The report's blanket statement that much TP4 content was reviewed in 2023 was not independently established from the retrieved visible page text. A public page remaining online does not establish that it controls a new project.

The optional reference uses source links and original synthesis. It carries no CDC marks, copied agency shell, or claim of endorsement. Keep source check dates distinct from source publication dates. Recheck mutable package, policy, and template facts at the time of an actual implementation.
